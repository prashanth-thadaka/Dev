# Veehoster design system

Veehoster provides hosting, domain registration, website development and SEO. Currency: INR. Contact: +91 96404 69666, info@veehoster.com.

## Direction

Confident, spacious and approachable. Warm ivory canvases, navy type, electric violet calls to action and restrained lime details. Manrope headings and DM Sans body text are self-hosted. Bootstrap 5.3.8 supplies responsive grid and form foundations; the visual design is custom.

The homepage combines an editorial hero, domain search, asymmetrical service panels, numbered differentiators, hosting plans, a migration journey, a cPanel preview, project showcases, FAQ, contact CTA and large footer. Hosting uses product comparisons, website development emphasizes portfolio and process, domains emphasize search, and SEO explains deliverables and measurement.

Account screens use a dedicated sidebar and information hierarchy. Customer services, domains, invoices and support reflect database records. Administration covers customers, orders, quotations and support requests. Payment and domain availability are visibly labeled as demonstrations.
